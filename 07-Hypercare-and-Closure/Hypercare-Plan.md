# Hypercare & Handover Plan

## Hypercare objectives
- Stabilize critical business processes.
- Rapidly triage production incidents.
- Monitor integrations, data and user adoption.
- Transfer ownership to steady-state support.

## Handover criteria
- [ ] Support teams trained
- [ ] Runbooks/knowledge articles available
- [ ] Vendor escalation route confirmed
- [ ] Known errors/workarounds documented
- [ ] Open project defects transferred with owners
- [ ] Operational KPIs agreed
- [ ] Business owners accept transition
- [ ] Closure actions recorded
