# Project Charter — Finance & Procurement ERP Transformation

**Case study type:** Simulated / illustrative  
**Project:** Northstar Services ERP Transformation  
**Delivery lead:** Project Manager  
**Planned duration:** 9 months  
**Business areas:** Finance, Procurement, Operations  
**Solution approach:** Commercial off-the-shelf ERP (COTS), configuration-first

## Business problem
Northstar Services uses fragmented spreadsheets and legacy applications for purchasing, approvals, supplier records and financial reporting. Manual handoffs create duplicate data entry, inconsistent controls, limited audit visibility and slow month-end reporting.

## Objectives
| Objective | Target measure |
|---|---|
| Standardize procure-to-pay | 90%+ of routine purchasing follows approved ERP workflow |
| Improve financial close | Reduce month-end close cycle from 10 to 6 business days |
| Improve control visibility | 100% of configured approval workflows have documented owners |
| Reduce manual duplication | Remove agreed duplicate-entry steps from priority processes |
| Establish support ownership | Production support and escalation model approved before go-live |

## In scope
- General ledger and financial reporting
- Accounts payable
- Supplier master
- Requisitions, purchase orders and approvals
- Selected integrations
- Data migration for agreed master/open transaction data
- Role/access design coordination
- UAT, training/readiness, cutover and hypercare

## Out of scope
- Payroll replacement
- CRM replacement
- Bespoke mobile application development
- Historical-data migration beyond the agreed retention scope

## Success criteria
- Critical requirements validated in UAT.
- No unresolved Severity 1 defects at go-live.
- Data reconciliation meets approved thresholds.
- Business process owners approve readiness.
- Cutover, support and rollback/contingency plans are approved.

## Governance
| Forum | Purpose | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Steering Committee | Decisions, escalations, scope/budget oversight | Monthly |
| Project Core Team | Cross-workstream delivery and dependencies | Weekly |
| Design / Fit-Gap | Process and solution decisions | Twice weekly during design |
| RAID Review | Risks, issues, assumptions and dependencies | Weekly |
| Cutover Command | Go-live execution and escalation | Daily during cutover/hypercare |

> All organizations, figures and scenarios in this repository are fictional and created for portfolio demonstration.
